## 🚀 Summary
Fusion 360 for Windows is a single environment that carries a design from its first sketch through engineering validation and on to manufacturing. Because modeling, CAM, and simulation share the same data, changes ripple through your whole project instead of stranding you between separate files. It scales from a weekend hobby build to serious product development, giving everyone the same connected toolkit.

## ✨ Feature Set
1. Parametric and freeform modeling for precise, editable geometry 📐
2. Integrated CAM for milling, turning, and cutting toolpaths ⚙️
3. Simulation studies for stress, motion, and thermal checks 🔬
4. Assemblies and joints that reveal how parts move together 🧩
5. Cloud storage with version history and easy sharing ☁️

## 📥 Installation Steps
1. Click the **Download** button above to get the latest version of Fusion 360.
2. Run the downloaded installer and complete the on-screen setup.
3. Open Fusion 360 and sign in with your Autodesk account, or create a new one.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Start a new design and open a sketch on your chosen plane.
2. Draw a base profile, then build a solid with Extrude or Revolve.
3. Add fillets, patterns, and joints to develop the design.
4. Use the Manufacture and Simulation workspaces to program toolpaths or run tests.
5. Save the project to the cloud to keep it backed up and ready to share.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is Fusion 360 free?**
Yes, for the right use: Autodesk Fusion 360 is free for eligible personal and hobby projects and free for students and educators, while commercial and professional work requires a paid subscription. The free tier is intended for non-commercial users.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
Fusion 360 works on modern 64-bit Windows, so both Windows 10 and Windows 11 are supported.

**Is my data secure?**
Projects are stored in Autodesk's cloud with sign-in protection, version history, and per-project sharing controls that let you decide who has access.

## 🛟 Support
For help with Fusion 360, open the built-in Help or Support section inside the app, where you'll find documentation, tutorials, troubleshooting tips, and contact options. You can also consult the official Autodesk Fusion 360 website for the newest guides and community support.
